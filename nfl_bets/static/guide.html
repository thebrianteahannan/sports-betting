1 The parrot in the corner

"Tap me. I'll show you something you haven't tried yet."

The parrot sits in the top right corner. Leave the page alone for a bit and a speech bubble pops out next to him with a tip you have not used yet. Tap the bubble and he takes you there, or lights up the button he is talking about.

Once you use something, he stops bringing it up. When you have tried everything, he goes quiet.

On a phone, tap the bubble once to read the whole tip, then tap it again to go.

2 Your profile

"Tap the little person in the corner. Your record and your settings live in your profile."

The person button sits in the bottom right corner, next to your name. Tap it to open your profile. Tap it again to go back to the desk.

  • Your name: what the parrot calls you.
  • Your state: the desk only shows bets you can place where you live.
  • Your sportsbook: the book you bet with.
  • Your teams: so Recommended on the home screen can show only your teams' games.
  • Your friends: the people you can send bets to.

3 Share the site with a QR code

"Double-tap me and I'll put a QR code on the TV. Your friends scan it and they're in."

Double-tap the parrot in the top right corner. A QR code fills the TV. Anyone in the room points their phone camera at it and lands on parlayoftheday.com. Tap anywhere to close it.

4 Find the games: today and this week

"Every game today and this week is on The Games. Tap here and I'll take you."

On Build a bet, tap The Games in the top right. You can also tap The Games on the parrot's first screen.

The Games has three tabs on the right:

  • Past: games that are over, with how the recommended bets did.
  • Today: every game today, with scores live while they play.
  • This week: everything coming up this week.

The league buttons on the left (All, NBA, NFL, NHL) narrow it down. Tap Build a bet on any game to start a bet on it.

5 Show or hide Recommended bets

"On The Games, flip on Recommended bets and my picks show up on every game."

At the top of The Games, check Recommended bets. Every game the parrot likes gets a green Recommended tag and a row of his picks under it. Tap a pick to open it in Build a bet. Uncheck it to hide them and just watch the scores.

The scoreboard next to it keeps his record: how his picks are doing live, today, and all time.

See the picture in section 4: the checkbox is circled at the top.

6 The parrot icons on players

"See my face next to a player? Tap it and I'll tell you why I like him."

Open any game in Build a bet. Under Player bets, the players the parrot recommends have a little parrot face next to their name. Tap it to jump to his pick for that player, with the reason, the price, and what the record has to clear.

7 The + button on players

"Tap the + on a player to throw him in your bet now. Pick his prop later."

Every player in a game has a + next to his name. Tap it and he goes into THE BET right away, before you pick a prop. The + turns into a check mark. Tap it again to take him out.

When you are ready, tap his name in THE BET to choose the prop (see section 8).

8 Change a leg in THE BET

THE BET is the bet you are building. Every leg in it can be changed right where it is.

Tap the name to change the prop

"Tap a player's name in your bet to swap him to another prop."

Tap a player's name and every prop he has comes up: yards, receptions, touchdowns, and more. Tap one to swap it in.

Tap the prop to change the number

"Tap the prop in your bet to move the number up or down."

Tap the prop (the gold words) and the other lines come up with their prices and chances. A lower number hits more often and pays less. A higher number pays more.

9 The leg buttons: trends, swaps, move, search

Every player leg has a row of small buttons on the right. On a phone, tap ⋯ to see them.

Magnifier: searchPulls up everything on that player: every prop, every game.
Chart: trendsHow often he has cleared this exact bet in his recent games, this season, and his career.
List: add to another betPuts this leg into another of your saved bets too.
Arrows: swapOther bets at about the same price, if you want a different player.

10 Paste a friend's bet from a screenshot

"Friend sent you a screenshot of a bet? Tap here, paste it, and I'll build it for you."

  • Copy the screenshot (from a text, a group chat, or your betting app).
  • On Build a bet, tap Paste a bet at the top right.
  • Paste it (⌘V on a Mac, Ctrl+V on Windows), or tap Choose a file.

The parrot reads the legs and builds the bet in THE BET, ready to change, check, or share.

11 Send a bet to a friend

"Tap the share arrow on your bet to send it straight to a friend."

  • Tap the share arrow, the green box at the top right of THE BET.
  • Your bet turns into a card on the TV.
  • Tap Send to a friend, check the friends you want, add a note if you like, and tap Send.
  • Or tap Copy to paste the card into a text or a post.

Your friends see it under Shared in Saved bets. To add friends, open your profile.

12 Look back at how your bets did

"Want to know how your old bets went? Tap here and I'll show you what hit and what missed."

Your bets

On Build a bet, under Saved bets, tap Finished. Your settled bets are grouped by day and by game.

Open a game to see the whole story: what the parrot expected going in, what actually happened, the box score, and why each leg hit or missed.

The parrot's picks

On The Games, tap All time in the scoreboard at the top. You get every game the parrot recommended. Each pick shows what the player had against what the bet needed, and a gold 1 short tag marks the near misses. Open a game for the full review, and tap any pick for why it hit or missed.
