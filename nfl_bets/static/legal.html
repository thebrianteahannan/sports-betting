Privacy Policy

We collect what we need to run your membership, and we don't sell it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email, password (stored scrambled, never in plain text), date of birth, and state.
  • Your settings: what the parrot calls you, your sportsbook, and your favorite teams.
  • What you build: saved bets, finished bets, the friends you add, and bets you send or receive.
  • How you use the site: players and games you search for and the bets you build. We use this to improve the site, and the people who run it can see it.
  • What you send us to read: a screenshot of a bet you paste, or the words of a bet you type or speak.
  • Technical basics: a sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in for 30 days, settings saved in your browser, and normal web server logs such as your IP address and browser type.

How we use it

To sign you in, check that you're 21, show only the bets available in your state, save your bets, send bets to your friends, find your picks, grade bets after the games, keep the site secure, and make it better.
