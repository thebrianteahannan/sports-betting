What doing it yourself really takes

Say you want one good player bet for tonight, and you use ChatGPT or Claude to help. Here is the job:

  1. Open your sportsbook and copy tonight's player props and prices. The chatbot usually doesn't have today's lines, and when it guesses, the line or the price can be wrong.
  2. Look up each player's last 10 games and count how many times he cleared that exact number. Six rebounds is a different bet than five.
  3. Turn the price into the hit rate it needs. +122 needs 45%. −192 needs 65.8%. Get that wrong and a good-looking bet loses money over time.
  4. Check who is out or doubtful tonight, and how that changes who gets the minutes, the carries, or the shots.
  5. Check the matchup: does this opponent give up more rebounds, yards, or shots to his position than usual?
  6. Make sure the bet is one you can place in your state.
  7. For a parlay, multiply the chances together. Four legs that each hit 85% of the time come out to about a coin flip.
  8. Do it again for every game, every night, because the prices move all day.
  9. Tomorrow, look up the box scores to see what hit and what missed.

That's an hour or more of copying and pasting for one bet, and the chatbot can still slip on the math or invent a stat.
