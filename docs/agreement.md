# Draft: payback, then 50/50

This is a term sheet for a lawyer. It is not a signed contract, and it is not in force. It does not create a partnership, a marketing role, or a right to be paid. Both parties have to be 21 or older. A lawyer should turn these points into the real agreement before anyone signs.

## Parties

- Brian Hannan.
- The second party, by full legal name: ____________________.

Each person represents that they are 21 or older on the day they sign. If that is not true, this draft does not apply to them, and they are not a partner, a marketer, or a payee.

## What the money is for

Parlay of the Day (parlayoftheday.com) is a FanDuel price desk. It does not send bets and it is not a sportsbook. A later paid membership, if one is sold, is access to the board. It is not a promise that the picks will win.

The graded record as of October 9, 2026 is 83 wins and 219 losses. The signed agreement has to let that record, including losses, stay on the page.

## Order of money

1. Brian writes down cash he spends to get the desk running, with the date and what it paid for. That list is the launch cash.
2. Revenue is money the desk actually collects, after refunds and payment fees.
3. Launch cash is paid back to Brian from that revenue before any split. Repayment comes from money collected. It is not a promise that the picks will produce a profit, and it is not a debt the second party has to cover from their own pocket if revenue never arrives.
4. After the written launch cash has been paid back, what remains of net revenue is split evenly, 50/50, between the two adults who signed.
5. Net revenue means collected revenue minus the written costs of running the desk (hosting, data, payment fees, refunds, ads, and similar costs the two parties agree to list). Owner pay is not taken before the payback.

If there is no revenue, there is nothing to repay and nothing to split.

## What each person does

Brian runs the desk, keeps the launch-cash list, and keeps the graded record public.

The second party’s role is written here before anyone signs: ____________________.

A marketing role, if one is written in, is a job for an adult. It does not include placing bets, promising wins, or calling a ticket a lock.

## What this draft does not do

- It does not put the agreement in force.
- It does not authorize charging members. Charging waits on the checks in the business model.
- It does not guarantee members, revenue, or profit.
- It does not make either person a sportsbook, and it does not send bets.
- It does not include anyone under 21.

## Before it can be signed

A lawyer drafts the real document from these points. Both people are 21 or older, use their legal names, and sign that document. Until then, Brian is repaid nothing, and there is no 50/50.
