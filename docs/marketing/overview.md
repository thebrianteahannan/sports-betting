# Parlay of the Day

Short lines for a profile, a link post, or a first message. The name on the site is Parlay of the Day. The address to share is parlayoftheday.com.

## One sentence

Parlay of the Day is a FanDuel price desk that shows a few parlays, lets you build one, and keeps the graded record on the page.

## Slightly longer

The board reads FanDuel prices and lays out Today's Best Bet, Bet of the Week, and a handful of strategy cards. You can build a parlay from the leagues and prop you pick, swipe through suggestions, and rate or save a ticket on a free membership. Live games show the count on each leg. Wins and losses stay on the record. The desk does not send the bet. You type it into your own FanDuel account if you want it. 21+. If gambling is a problem, call 1-800-GAMBLER.

## What not to say

- Do not say the picks are locks, guaranteed, or consistently profitable.
- Do not quote a member count or a revenue number as if it has happened.
- Do not say the site places bets or is part of FanDuel.
- The graded record as of October 9, 2026 is 83 wins and 219 losses. If you mention results, use the record that is on the site that day, including losses.
