# Business model

This is a proposal for a later paid board. Nothing here is on sale today. The desk does not take payments, and it does not send bets. The member counts and profit ranges below are arithmetic from a $19.99 price. They are not a forecast.

## What would be sold

Access to the daily FanDuel board: Today's Best Bet, Bet of the Week, the strategy cards, Build a bet, swipe suggestions, live counts, and the graded archive. A free view, if one is offered, shows the public record. A paid view shows the full board.

The product is not a sportsbook. The reader still types any ticket into their own FanDuel account.

## Price used in the proposal

$19.99 per month, billed while the person stays subscribed.

| Paying members | Revenue in a full year |
| --- | ---: |
| 100 | $23,988 |
| 500 | $119,940 |
| 1,000 | $239,880 |
| 5,000 | $1,199,400 |

A pitch that rounds these to $24,000, $120,000, $240,000, and $1.2 million is the same price times 12. Hitting those totals requires that many people to pay every month for a year.

## What the pitch assumed about profit

At 1,000 paying members the pitch suggested about $100,000 to $170,000 of operating profit before taxes and owner pay, after marketing, data, and other costs. That range was not built from a budget. It depends on costs this repo does not yet have: ads, payment fees, refunds, support, and a lawyer's review before anyone is charged.

A lean Hostinger and Supabase setup is cheap next to a sportsbook. Cheap hosting does not make the picks profitable, and it does not create the members.

## What has to be true first

The graded record as of October 9, 2026 is 83 wins and 219 losses, 27.5% of decided tickets, with 118 still open. The page has to keep showing that record, including the losses. A subscription sold on "consistent value" or "reliable profit" would describe a result this board has not produced.

No one should be charged until:

- the record stays visible next to the picks
- the states where the site will be offered have been checked
- age is limited to 21+
- refund terms are written down
- upfront cash spent to launch is written down as it is spent

## How a later split was requested

Brian Hannan's request is that cash he puts in to get the desk running is paid back from revenue before anyone splits what remains, and that what remains after that payback is split 50/50.

That payback is a business term between adults. It is not in force. The points for a lawyer are written in [the draft agreement](agreement.md): Brian is repaid documented launch cash first, repayment comes from revenue not from a promise of profit, and only after that repayment is net money split evenly. Both people who sign have to be 21 or older. A 50/50, a marketing role, or a payment from this project is not available to anyone under 21.

## What a small edge looks like

A single $1 bet at −110 wins $0.91 and loses $1.00. It has to win 52.4% of the time to break even. That line is for one side of one price. The parlays on this board are a different bet, and their graded rate is 27.5%.

| Win rate on $1 bets at −110 | Expected on 100 bets |
| --- | ---: |
| 50.0% | −$4.55 |
| 52.4% | $0 |
| 54.0% | +$3.09 |
| 55.0% | +$5.00 |
| 57.0% | +$8.82 |

Two $1 bets a week at a 54% win rate expect about 6 cents a week. That is the shape of a small edge on a $20 bank. It is not a plan to turn $20 into serious money on a season of $1 bets. A hedge at −110 against −110 locks about a 9 cent loss on $1 a side, because both prices include the vig.

The 150-member row was the target in that earlier note: $3,000 a month before the card fee, the lawyer, support, refunds, and time. The 10,000-member row is arithmetic. It is not step one. Costs already paid, and costs still to pay, come out before anyone splits what is left.

## Before anyone is charged

Form an LLC, get an EIN, and open a business bank account. Business money stays separate from personal money. Sell the board. The company does not take the bet. Keep every pick: the odds, the date, the legs, and the result, including losses.

A lawyer who knows Tennessee gambling rules and online subscriptions reads the site, the ads, and any affiliate deal before the first charge. Sportsbook affiliate links wait for that review. The order of work is the paper record, the lawyer, the company papers, HTTPS, the 21+ check, an honest application to a processor, and only then a charge to adults.
